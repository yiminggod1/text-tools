Privacy Policy

Text Tools is a free collection of browser-based utilities. For the core tools, text processing is performed in your browser. You do not need to create an account to use the main utilities.

Information processed by the tools

Text entered into a core text utility is processed by the page running in your browser. Where a tool explicitly uses an external service in the future, that behavior will be described on the relevant page.

Advertising, cookies and analytics

Text Tools may use Google or other advertising and analytics services after those services are enabled. Third-party providers may use cookies, device identifiers, IP address information, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, personalize, or protect their services, subject to their own policies and applicable consent choices.

When advertising is enabled, advertisements are intended to support the free availability of the site. Users should not click advertisements merely to support the site, and ad placements are designed to remain separate from the controls used to operate the text tools.

Consent and regional requirements

Where applicable law requires consent for cookies or personalized advertising, the site may present a consent or privacy-choice mechanism. Available choices can vary by location, browser, service configuration, and applicable law.

Third-party links

The site may link to external websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers.

Children and sensitive information

The tools are general-purpose utilities. Do not enter passwords, financial information, confidential business material, or other sensitive information into a shared computer or an external service unless you understand the service's data handling.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact page.
